* ->Symptoms can begin within minutes to an hour after exposure, depending on the dose and mode of contact. Death can occur within 30 minutes to a few hours in severe cases due to respiratory failure or cardiovascular collapse.
* ->Symptoms include:
--->Immediate nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, and abdominal pain.
--->Headache, dizziness, and rapid heart rate.
--->Severe exposure can lead to seizures, low blood pressure, loss of consciousness, and respiratory failure.
--->The victim may feel confusion and a rapid deterioration in physical ability.
* ->Sodium azide disrupts cellular respiration, leading to oxygen deprivation at the cellular level. After death, there are no distinctive physical signs on the body. The compound itself is not corrosive, and any residual traces degrade over time.
* ->Sodium azide can be detected in blood, urine, and tissues through specialized toxicological tests, but it requires specific suspicion to be identified. Routine autopsy procedures are unlikely to detect it unless the symptoms or circumstances suggest poisoning.
* ->Sodium azide poisoning mimics cyanide poisoning in its effects, as both disrupt cellular oxygen usage. It can also be mistaken for other forms of respiratory failure or sudden cardiac arrest without toxicology testing.
